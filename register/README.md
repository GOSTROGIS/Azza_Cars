# Brand register

`brands.csv` is the list of automotive brands that Azza Cars needs logos for. It is
generated by `tools/register/build_register.py` from `candidates.csv`. Do not edit it by hand.

## Scope (agreed with the owner, 2026-11-22)

Every automotive brand sold in North America (United States, Canada, Mexico),
current **and** discontinued, passenger vehicles **and** trucks.

The work is phased so that nothing is dropped:

| Phase | What | Status |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Brands that sold cars, SUVs, pickups or vans new through dealer networks since 1981 (the 17-digit VIN era covered by NHTSA vPIC), plus on-highway truck brands | `candidates.csv`, 158 brands |
| 2 | Pre-1981 discontinued brands (Studebaker, Packard, DeSoto, Edsel, Nash, Hudson, Kaiser, Willys, Acadian, Beaumont, Meteor, Monarch, ...) | not started |
| 3 | Low-volume, kit, replica, coachbuilder, bus, RV, fire-apparatus and specialty makes in the NHTSA registry (about 12,400 makes of every vehicle type) | catalogued in `sources/vpic_getallmakes.json`, not yet reviewed |

## How each brand is confirmed

No brand is in the register on memory alone. Each row is checked against three
independent public sources, and `sources_confirming` counts how many agree:

1. **NHTSA vPIC**: US federal make registry. Column `vpic_make_id`.
2. **INEGI RAIAVL**: Mexico's official light-vehicle sales register, 2005 onward,
   reported per company. Column `inegi_years`.
3. **Wikidata**: via the English Wikipedia article. Columns `wikidata_qid`,
   `wikidata_logo_files` (property P154), `wikidata_websites` (P856),
   `wikidata_inception`, `wikidata_dissolved`.

Raw responses, the SPARQL queries and SHA-256 hashes are kept in `sources/`
(`provenance.json`). The INEGI zip (about 22 MB) is not committed. Its URL and hash are.

## Known limits

- **No brand has zero sources.** Four have no Wikidata item: Tank and Ora (Great
  Wall sub-brands confirmed by INEGI model records "TANK 300" and "ORA 03"), and
  Sterling cars and WhiteGMC (confirmed by vPIC).
- **INEGI reports by company, not brand.** "General Motors" covers Chevrolet, GMC,
  Buick, Cadillac, Pontiac, Hummer and Saab. "Chrysler" covers Chrysler, Dodge,
  Jeep and Ram. A sub-brand only counts as INEGI-confirmed when model records start
  with its name (column `inegi_model_prefix`, e.g. `TANK` matches "TANK 300"). The
  matched records are listed in `inegi_models_matched`, and `inegi_years` spans only
  those records. That is a lower bound: GMC shows 2006-2019 because only its
  "GMC Sierra" records carry the prefix. Brands whose models carry no prefix
  (Chevrolet, Dodge, Buick, Cadillac) rely on vPIC and Wikidata.
- **Not every Mexican brand reports to INEGI.** BYD, for example, does not appear.
- **Wikidata dates describe the company entity, not always the brand.** Example:
  Jaguar shows "dissolved 2012" (the old Jaguar Cars Ltd merged into Jaguar Land
  Rover), yet the brand is active. Brand status is therefore not taken from
  Wikidata alone.
- **Canada has no public per-brand sales register.** Canada-only brands (Asüna,
  Passport, Lada) are confirmed through vPIC and Wikidata.
