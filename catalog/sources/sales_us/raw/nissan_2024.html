NASHVILLE, Tenn. – Nissan Group announced total U.S. fourth quarter (October - December) sales for 2024 of 222,484 units, an increase of 10.3% versus the prior year. The increase is due to strong sales of Nissan models across the lineup, including the all-new Kicks, Versa and Pathfinder.

Nissan Group also announced total calendar year 2024 U.S. sales of 924,008 units, an increase of 2.8% compared to the prior year.

Nissan Division fourth quarter 2024 highlights:

  • Sales of the Nissan Kicks compact crossover were up 51.1% year-over-year for the quarter. Kicks set sales records in all three months for the quarter.
  • Sales of the Nissan Versa sedan were up 91% year-over-year for the quarter.
  • Sales of the three-row Nissan Pathfinder crossover were up 44.2% year-over-year for the quarter.
  • Sales of the Nissan Sentra sedan were up 43.7% year-over-year for the quarter.
  • Sales of the Nissan Ariya electric crossover were up 30.2% year-over-year for the quarter.
  • Sales of the Nissan Frontier midsize pickup were up 13.5% year-over-year for the quarter.

Nissan Division 2024 year-end highlights:

  • Sales of Ariya were up 47% for the calendar year, the best sales year since the vehicle's launch in 2022.
  • Sales of Versa were up 71.7% for the calendar year.
  • Sales of Sentra were up 39.8% for the calendar year.
  • Sales of Frontier were up 17.2% for the calendar year.
  • Sales of the Pathfinder were up 12.8% for the calendar year.

With the new year, Nissan is celebrating the arrival of the all-new 2025 Armada and Murano at dealerships across the U.S.

*INFINITI sales total included for reference. For more information on INFINITI's fourth-quarter and year-end sales performance, please visit INFINITINews.com.

NOTE: To ensure consistency in global sales reporting, Nissan North America calculates quarterly variances on a straight-percentage basis, unadjusted for the number of selling days. Fourth quarter 2024 had 78 selling days and fourth quarter 2023 had 76 selling days. Calendar year 2024 had 308 selling days and calendar year 2023 had 306 selling days.
