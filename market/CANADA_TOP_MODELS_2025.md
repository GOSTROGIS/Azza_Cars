# Best-selling new vehicles in Canada, calendar year 2025

Retrieved: 2026-11-23 (all rows). Market total, 2025: 1,897,058 units (Automotive News Data Center, as cited by The Car Guide).

Type key:
- **MFR**: from the manufacturer's own release.
- **3P**: from a third party (Automotive News Data Center via The Car Guide/CarGurus, GoodCarBadCar (GCBC), GM Authority, Ford Authority).
- **3P-est**: a GM model total. GM Canada's public year-end release gives no model figures (only the 299,813 total), so every GM model number comes from a third party.

Source short codes (full URLs below):
- **GA10**: The Car Guide top 10.
- **GAB**: The Car Guide gallery of each brand's best seller.
- **GCBC**: goodcarbadcar.net/{model}-sales-figures/, Canada section.
- **FCA**: FCA Canada Q4/full-year 2025 release.
- **MAZ**: Mazda Canada full-year 2025 release.
- **KIA**: Kia Canada 2025 release.
- **FORD**: Ford Canada 2025 highlights.
- **TCI**: Toyota Canada 2025 release.
- **HON**: The Car Guide, Honda/Toyota 2025.
- **MB**: Mercedes-Benz Canada 2025 release.
- **BSCB**: Best Selling Cars Blog, Canada full year 2025 (ranks and % changes only; units paywalled).

## Unresolved items

1. **Ford Transit 2025 full-year Canada total.** Not found. Possible sources: Ford Canada (no public model table), GoodCarBadCar (Canada data members-only), and the Ford Authority Q3 and Q4 2025 Transit articles (blocked).
2. **Full-year Express and Savana totals.** These are GCBC figures only. GM Canada's public release has no model figures, and the GM Authority Q4 article could not be opened directly.
3. **Models that may belong in the top 50 but have no figure.** Honda HR-V, Subaru Outback, VW Atlas, Kia Carnival, Toyota Prius, Mitsubishi RVR, Lexus models other than NX and RX. GCBC Canada data for these is members-only or missing. Honda Canada publishes no model table beyond Civic and CR-V. **HR-V in particular is likely in the top 30, so ranks 14–50 are provisional.**
4. **Paywalled full rankings.** Focus2Move "Top 50" returns 403, and the Best Selling Cars Blog table is for Platinum members only. Driving.ca could not be searched; its search tool returns 400.
5. **Unreconciled disagreements:**
   - Sierra: 59,962 vs 61,400.
   - Silverado: 54,068 vs 56,824. Its rank (#4 or #5) is also disputed.
   - Equinox: 25,198 vs 15,139.
   - Outlander: 29,629 vs 12,688.
   - Audi Q5: 14,998 vs 15,998.
   - Model Y: 13,057 vs 12,965.
   - CR-V: 55,987 vs 57,987 (likely a typo).
6. **Automotive News Canada headline.** It reads "CR-V dethrones Ford F-150, Toyota RAV4 as supply shortages hand Honda top spot". It was not opened; it probably refers to a single month, not 2025.
7. **2026/2027 discontinuation checks.** These come from US sources (Cars.com, Edmunds) and are not confirmed for Canada.
   - Confirmed in the list: Ford Escape (ended), Chrysler Pacifica Hybrid (2025 was the last model year), Nissan Kicks Play (not offered for 2026), Kia Soul, Ford Edge, Transit Connect, ProMaster City.
   - Not checked: Hyundai Venue, Nissan Versa and Sentra in Canada, Mitsubishi RVR.
8. **Market total discrepancy.** Automotive News gives 1,897,058; GCBC gives 1,866,111 or 1,863,837 on the same page; Statistics Canada gives 1,958,582 (all new motor vehicles).
