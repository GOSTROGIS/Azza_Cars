(Kenneth Chan) Vancouver International Airport (YVR) reached all-time historic annual highs in both passenger traffic and freight cargo volumes in 2025, cementing its position as Canada’s second-busiest airport and its role as a key gateway between North America and Asia. Even with the climate of economic uncertainty and lower immigration, the airport welcomed 26.914 million passengers last year, representing a 2.7 per cent increase from 26.205 million passengers in 2024 and the highest total in YVR’s 94-year history. The previous record of 26.38 million passengers was set in pre-pandemic 2019.
