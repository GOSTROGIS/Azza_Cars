# Marketing review: car, van and truck rental in Metro Vancouver

Prepared 22 November 2026 for the launch of the business working as "Azza Cars". Every figure below carries a link to its source. Where a claim could not be verified, the text says so. Recommendations that rest on judgement rather than a source are labelled as such.

## The answer

A small independent operator in Metro Vancouver should not try to out-advertise Enterprise, Hertz or Avis Budget. It should win the searches that the majors serve badly: local, high-intent, neighbourhood searches for vans and trucks, plus car renters who want a clear all-in price and a fast handover. Five moves, in order:

1. **Own the map pack before spending on ads.** Google ranks local results on relevance, distance and prominence, and review count and score feed prominence ([Google Business Profile help](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)). One verified profile per real location, a name that matches the signage, and a steady flow of reviews is the cheapest demand engine available.
2. **Lead with vans and trucks, not airport cars.** Google autocomplete for Metro Vancouver shows neighbourhood, hourly-job and moving intent ("cargo van rental burnaby", "moving van rental north vancouver", "home depot van rental burnaby") (raw responses in [sources/google_autocomplete.txt](sources/google_autocomplete.txt)). The airport is crowded with on-site majors in the YVR parkade ([YVR](https://www.yvr.ca/en/passengers/transportation/car-rentals)).
3. **Sell all-in prices.** Drip pricing is explicitly unlawful in Canada since June 2022, government-imposed charges excepted ([Competition Bureau](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2022/06/important-amendments-to-the-competition-act-come-into-effect.html)), and car rental companies have already paid a total of $5.95 million in penalties over drip pricing ([Competition Bureau, 2018](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2018/10/discount-car-rental-penalised-for-advertising-unattainable-prices.html)). A transparent price is both a legal floor and a positioning lever against the majors.
4. **Build B2B accounts by hand, but do not count on ICBC direct billing.** ICBC's preferred rental suppliers, effective 1 September 2026, are Budget and Enterprise ([ICBC partners](https://partners.icbc.com/material-damage/rental-vehicles)), and only preferred suppliers can bill ICBC directly ([ICBC](https://icbc.com/claims/repair-replace/Rental-cars-and-other-transportation)). Body shops, dealerships and trade businesses remain open, but the insurance-replacement prize that built Enterprise is largely closed to a newcomer in BC.
5. **Use marketplaces for fill, not for identity.** Rentalcars.com Marketplace, Discover Cars, Expedia and Turo all accept smaller suppliers, which gives a new fleet utilisation on day one. Their customers belong to the platform, so the brand must still be built through search, reviews and repeat customers.

**Synthesis.** The majors segment by price tier and by airport versus neighbourhood. Car share segments by trip length. U-Haul segments by job (moving). Nobody in this list positions on a fast, transparent, design-led handover for mixed cars, vans and trucks in the neighbourhoods. That gap is a judgement drawn from the table, not a sourced market measurement.

## 3. Channels

### 3.1 Local SEO and Google Business Profile

- Local ranking rests on relevance, distance and prominence. Google says prominence draws on links, articles and directories, and that "review count and review score factor into local search ranking". It also says there is no way to pay for a better local ranking ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)).
- The profile name must match the real-world name. Google states that "including unnecessary information in your business name isn't permitted" and that this can lead to suspension. Its examples reject taglines ("TD Bank, America's Most Convenient Bank") and location add-ons ("Equinox near SOHO") ([Google guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). Keyword-stuffing the profile name is therefore not an option, which matters for naming.
- A delivery-only operation may use one service-area profile and must hide its address ([Google guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)).
- Autocomplete shows that people attach the city or neighbourhood to the query: Burnaby, Metrotown, Kingsway, Richmond, Surrey Central, Coquitlam, North Vancouver and Marine Drive ([sources/google_autocomplete.txt](sources/google_autocomplete.txt)). Recommendation (judgement): build one useful landing page per served city and per vehicle type (car, cargo van, pickup, moving truck), each with real inventory, pickup details and all-in prices.
- Google's own starter guide says keywords in a domain or URL path "alone have hardly any effect" beyond breadcrumbs, and a country TLD only matters when targeting a specific country ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)). A .ca domain is a reasonable signal for a Canada-only business but not a ranking shortcut.

### 3.2 Search ads

- Location assets show the address and phone number in ads and can show a "Call" button on mobile ([Google Ads help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2404182?hl=en)). This suits urgent van and truck jobs.
- Target ROAS bidding lets Google set bids to maximise conversion value at a target return, and Google warns that a target set too high limits traffic ([Google Ads help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6268637?hl=en)). Recommendation (judgement): start with manual or maximise-conversions bidding on a narrow set of exact and phrase terms, then move to value-based bidding once booking values flow back as conversions.
- Brand terms of competitors appear heavily in autocomplete ("enterprise car rental burnaby", "budget car rental richmond bc", "uhaul van rental burnaby"). Cost-per-click data could not be retrieved, so no CPC figure is given here.

### 3.3 OTAs and aggregators

- Rentalcars.com launched Marketplace in February 2019 for smaller rental companies, giving operators "full control of fleet, rates, discounts, and extras", with the supplier base managed for service quality ([Auto Rental News](https://www.autorentalnews.com/news/rentalcarscom-launches-marketplace-for-small-rental-businesses)). Whether the programme still runs in this form could not be confirmed.
- Discover Cars says it contracts directly with both international companies and "smaller, local businesses", and that choosing partners is a lengthy process ([Discover Cars](https://www.discovercars.com/blog/how-discover-cars-works)). Supplier commission terms are not published and could not be verified.
- Expedia Group runs a car rental supplier enquiry page ([Expedia Group partners](https://partner.expediagroup.com/en-au/industries/car-rentals)). Kayak appears in search results as a meta-search over suppliers, but no supplier-side terms were found.
- Recommendation (judgement): list on one or two marketplaces once insurance, deposits and the handover process are stable, cap the share of fleet sold through them, and use them to fill weekday and shoulder-season gaps. Treat a marketplace review as public evidence for the brand.

### 3.4 Corporate and insurance-replacement accounts

- ICBC Loss of Use coverage pays for a rental while a car is repaired, limited to "vehicles of a similar size", and "ICBC doesn't authorize electric vehicle rentals" at present ([ICBC](https://icbc.com/claims/repair-replace/Rental-cars-and-other-transportation)). Preferred suppliers with confirmed coverage can bill ICBC directly, and the preferred suppliers listed from 1 September 2026 are Budget and Enterprise ([ICBC partners](https://partners.icbc.com/material-damage/rental-vehicles)). How a non-preferred company is paid is not stated on that page. The likely path is customer reimbursement, which is slower and less attractive to the customer. That is an inference, not a confirmed rule.
- Corporate accounts are business-to-business rentals that depend on regular in-person selling. One operator quoted by Auto Rental News said more than 70% of his revenue came from corporate rentals ([Auto Rental News](https://www.autorentalnews.com/articles/how-to-increase-corporate-rental-sales)). The article is old and the figure is a single anecdote, not an industry average.
- Recommendation (judgement): target trades, film and event production, property managers and small contractors who need vans and pickups on account, with monthly invoicing and named-driver lists.

### 3.5 Partnerships: dealerships, body shops, hotels, cruise terminal

- Body shops: Enterprise's programme shows the model, a dedicated replacement desk and pickup at the shop ([Enterprise Canada](https://www.enterprise.ca/en/car-rental/insurance-replacement/replacement-resources/bodyshop-solutions.html)). An independent can offer the same to shops for customers outside ICBC direct billing, such as private-insurance, warranty and out-of-pocket repairs.
- Dealerships: no source was found on dealership loaner outsourcing in BC. Treat this as a hypothesis to test with three or four service managers.
- Hotels and the cruise terminal: no verified partnership terms were found. Demand exists (see section 2). Recommendation (judgement): offer delivery and collection at downtown hotels and Canada Place rather than seek a counter, and track bookings with a unique code per partner.
- Handover technology: Keycafe, based in Vancouver, sells key-management systems to rental firms including Avis, Hertz, Mobyl and U-Save ([Keycafe](https://www.keycafe.com/industries/car-rental)). Self-service handover at partner sites is feasible with off-the-shelf tools.

### 3.6 Referral programmes and reviews

- In Nielsen's 2021 Trust in Advertising study, 88% of global respondents trusted recommendations from people they know more than any other channel ([Nielsen](https://www.nielsen.com/insights/2021/beyond-martech-building-trust-with-consumers-and-engaging-where-sentiment-is-high/)).
- BrightLocal's 2025 survey found only 4% of consumers say they never read online reviews of businesses ([BrightLocal](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey-2025/)).
- Google's guidelines require that promotions and giveaways link clearly to their terms ([Google guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). Vendor guidance warns against paying for reviews ([Wheelbase](https://help.wheelbasepro.com/hc/en-us/articles/51830091430299-Setting-Up-Google-My-Business-for-Your-Car-Rental-Operation)), but that is a vendor statement, not Google policy text verified in this review.
- Recommendation (judgement): reward referrals (a credit for the referrer and the friend), never reviews. Ask every customer for a review at return, by text, with a direct link.

## 4. Pricing tactics

- **Price all-in.** Mandatory fees must be in the advertised price, and only government-imposed charges such as sales tax may be added ([Competition Bureau](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2022/06/important-amendments-to-the-competition-act-come-into-effect.html)). In BC that includes the Passenger Vehicle Rental Tax of $1.50 per day, which lessors collect on top of PST ([BC PST small business guide](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/taxes/sales-taxes/pst/publications/small-business-guide)). Older guidance limits it to rentals of 28 days or less, and the minimum-duration rule should be confirmed with the Ministry before launch.
- **Measure what the majors measure.** Hertz tracks Total RPD (revenue per transaction day) as its pricing measure and Vehicle Utilization alongside it ([Hertz 10-K FY2023](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785324000022/htz-20231231.htm)). A small fleet should report the same two numbers weekly per vehicle class.
- **Tier the fleet, not the brand.** Hertz and Avis Budget use separate brands for price tiers ([Hertz 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1657853/000165785325000015/htz-20241231.htm), [Avis Budget 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/723612/000072361225000005/car-20241231.htm)). A single new brand can offer good, better and best classes under one name.
- **Price by job for vans and trucks.** Autocomplete includes hourly-job and moving intent ("home depot van rental burnaby", "how much does it cost to rent a cargo van for a month") ([sources/google_autocomplete.txt](sources/google_autocomplete.txt)). Recommendation (judgement): offer hourly, half-day, day and monthly van and pickup rates, and weekly car rates for repair-period customers.
- **No price benchmarks are given.** No verified Vancouver rate data was gathered. Do a manual rate survey across Enterprise, Budget, U-Haul and two independents for the same dates before setting rates.

## 5. What works for a small independent

The pattern across the evidence:

1. Independents already show up in local search. Autocomplete for Richmond lists "routes car rental richmond bc" and "priceless car rental richmond bc" alongside the majors ([sources/google_autocomplete.txt](sources/google_autocomplete.txt)).
2. Local ranking cannot be bought ([Google](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en)), so reviews and real locations matter more than budget.
3. Marketplaces let small suppliers set their own rates and inventory ([Auto Rental News](https://www.autorentalnews.com/news/rentalcarscom-launches-marketplace-for-small-rental-businesses)), which reduces launch risk.
4. The insurance-replacement channel in BC is gated by ICBC preferred-supplier status ([ICBC partners](https://partners.icbc.com/material-damage/rental-vehicles)), so the B2B plan must lean on trades, production and private repairs.
5. Transparent pricing is mandatory and has been enforced against car rental companies ([Competition Bureau, 2018](https://www.canada.ca/en/competition-bureau/news/2018/10/discount-car-rental-penalised-for-advertising-unattainable-prices.html)), which turns a compliance duty into a brand promise.
