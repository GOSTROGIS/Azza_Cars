# Naming: an SEO-led search for a replacement to "Azza Cars"

Prepared 22 November 2026. Raw evidence is kept in [sources/](sources/): Google autocomplete responses, GoDaddy availability results and a social handle probe. Every external claim links to its source. Scores marked "judgement" are the author's assessment and are not sourced facts.

## The answer

**Recommend Keyhand** (brand), trading as "Keyhand" with the descriptor "Car, van and truck rental" in the site title and tagline.

- It was the only candidate where both the bare **keyhand.com** and **keyhand.ca** came back available from GoDaddy ([sources/godaddy_batch1.tsv](sources/godaddy_batch1.tsv)). Every other strong name needed a modifier such as "drive" or "rent".
- Web searches found no car rental or automotive business named Keyhand. The only KEYHAND trademark found is a dead U.S. registration for sporting goods, cancelled in 2001, reported by a third-party aggregator ([Markinton](https://markinton.com/trademark/keyhand-74330200)).
- It says what the brand does at its best moment, keys in hand, and it fits cars, vans and trucks equally. It is plain English, short, and has no ethnic or regional reading (judgement).
- As a coined compound it is not "clearly descriptive" of rental services, which is the bar under section 12(1)(b) of the Trademarks Act ([Trademarks Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/t-13/page-4.html)). That makes it far easier to protect than a keyword name.

Main risks: the X handle @keyhand is taken (alternatives @keyhandco, @keyhandrentals and @drivekeyhand were free), Keycafe is a Vancouver key-management company with a similar "Key" prefix in an adjacent industry ([Keycafe](https://www.keycafe.com/industries/car-rental)), and a formal CIPO and USPTO clearance search still has to be done by the owner or an agent. Details in section 5.

## 1. Brief

- Not too ethnic-sounding, memorable, sleek, premium but accessible.

### 2.3 What this means for the name

1. The money words are "rental", the vehicle type and the place. Nobody searches for a brand-new name, so the name itself will not capture generic demand. Pages, the Google profile and reviews do that work (see [MARKETING_REVIEW.md](MARKETING_REVIEW.md)).
2. "Drive" is a liability in Vancouver because of street names. "Rent" is clumsy in a brand name and "Rentals" is ambiguous in a city where "rentals" usually means apartments (judgement).
3. Avoid "Van" as a pun on Vancouver, because "van" is also a vehicle class the business rents (judgement).

### 2.4 Exact-match domain or brand name

| Option | For | Against |
|---|---|---|
| Exact-match domain, for example vancouvertruckrental.ca (available, see [sources/godaddy_batch3.tsv](sources/godaddy_batch3.tsv)) | Self-explanatory in ads and link text. | Google says keywords in the domain "alone have hardly any effect" on ranking ([Google Search Central](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide)). Google's 2012 EMD update targeted low-quality exact-match domains ([Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-emd-update-15776.html)). John Mueller has said a keyword domain gives no recognisable SEO advantage and can restrict growth into other terms ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/john-mueller-on-keyword-domain-names-and-branding-for-seo/504423/)). A descriptive name is hard to register as a trademark under s. 12(1)(b) ([Trademarks Act](https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/t-13/page-4.html)). It also caps the business at one vehicle type and one place. The best exact matches (vancouvercarrental.com and .ca) are already taken. |
| Brand name, for example keyhand.com | Ownable, protectable, extends to new cities and vehicle types. The Google profile can carry the real name only, so a keyword cannot be added there anyway ([Google guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en)). | Needs a descriptor line and content to explain what it is. |

**Decision:** brand name for the domain and legal name, keywords in page titles, headings, Google profile categories and services, and ad copy. Do not put "rent" or "drive" in the brand. If a descriptor is wanted in the domain later, redirect a keyword domain to the brand rather than lead with it.

## 5. Shortlist: top five with full evidence

### 5.1 Keyhand (recommended)

- **Meaning:** the handover, keys in the hand. Neutral English compound, two syllables, spelled as it sounds.
- **Domains:** keyhand.com available, keyhand.ca available, keyhand.co available ([batch 1](sources/godaddy_batch1.tsv), [batch 2](sources/godaddy_batch2.tsv)).
- **Web conflicts:** a search for Keyhand with car rental, rentals or automotive found no business of that name. The closest names were Key Car Rental in Saudi Arabia and Iceland and Keycafe in Vancouver ([Key Car Rental, CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/key-car-rental-co), [Key Car Rental, Guide to Iceland](https://guidetoiceland.is/car-rentals/key-car-rental/7618), [Keycafe](https://www.keycafe.com/industries/car-rental)). A search for Keyhand in Canada found no company of that name, and surfaced Keycafe's Vancouver address ([CB Insights, Keycafe](https://www.cbinsights.com/company/keycafe)).
- **Trademarks:** the only KEYHAND record found is U.S. serial 74330200, registration 1851140, class 28 sporting goods, owner Bonny Sports Corporation, cancelled 16 September 2001, per a third-party aggregator ([Markinton](https://markinton.com/trademark/keyhand-74330200)). Not confirmed on USPTO TSDR.
- **Social:** X @keyhand taken. @keyhandco, @keyhandrentals and @drivekeyhand free on X ([sources/social_probe.tsv](sources/social_probe.tsv)). Instagram, TikTok and Facebook inconclusive.
- **Risk:** Keycafe sells key-management systems to rental companies from Vancouver. Different word, different service, but the shared "Key" and the shared customer base justify a professional clearance opinion.
- **Brand fit (judgement):** works in carbon black on bone white with a blood-orange key-tag device, reads premium without a luxury price signal.

## 6. Trademark databases: what was and was not possible

- **CIPO (Canada):** the search page at [ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch](https://ised-isde.canada.ca/cipo/trademark-search/srch) loaded, but it is a form with no documented URL query pattern, so it could not be queried from this session. **Not searched directly.**
- **USPTO (U.S.):** [tmsearch.uspto.gov](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/) loaded, but a scripted query was blocked by this session's permission policy. **Not searched directly.**
- **Third-party aggregators** (Markinton, Justia) were reached through web search and are cited above. They are not authoritative.

**Before filing, the owner or a trademark agent should:**

1. On CIPO, search "KEYHAND" and "KEY HAND" in Trademark, all statuses, Nice classes 12 (vehicles), 35 (business services) and 39 (transport and vehicle rental), and review KEY-prefixed marks in class 39.
2. On USPTO, run the same search, and check TSDR for serial 74330200 to confirm it is dead.
3. Search the BC Registry for business names containing "Keyhand".
4. Register keyhand.com and keyhand.ca the same day as the decision.

## 7. Social handles: method and limits
